An outpost in [[Dolbuz Ghod]] ran by a dwarven corporation, the Iron Seal. It has an enterance on both sides, permitting visitors through either the outside walls, or the town itself. No guest status is considered here, and most dwarves in the town stay here. Outsiders should beware of salespersons offering generous rewards in exchange for a short work contract...
