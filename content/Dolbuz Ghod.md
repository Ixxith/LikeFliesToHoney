Dolbuz Ghod is an unique town, south of the [[North Iri Desert]]. While technically possessed by the Dolbuz Ghod Clan, one of the [[Clans of Bralgoh]], unlike the other clans, there is a high proportion of outsiders, especially from the Iri Dynasty. Many come to seek materials from the [[Underground Jungle Caverns]] to the north, while the Iri Dynstaty uses it as an outpost to seek labor, offering nebulous contracts, often signed by unwilling workers. 

The town is ruled by Chief [[Zarod Nargol]], whose family has gathered great wealth from the visitors and guest system. Many of the noble class in the town, known as the godskin, are considered children of divinity, tracing their lineage back to the ruling ancestral god of the tribe, Bashuk. Great care must be taken not to offend a Godskin, less one finds themselves offered as a blood sacrifice to the ancestral god.
