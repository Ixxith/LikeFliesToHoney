## Description

Capital city of the \[\[Jinsinlis Legionate\]\]. People here are very patriotic, and suspicious of outsiders and especially non-humanoids. Anyone thought to be a demon will find themselves quickly imprisoned and interrogated.

The city is broken up into six regions, for five, a hero that sacrificed their life defending the walls from the great demon invasion in the \[\[Abyssal Lands\]\], and for \[\[Tempus\]\], god of war. While visitors are distrusted, they are still afforded some liberties, such as non-eligibility for the national draft, and even honorary ranks for those with remarkable achievements.
