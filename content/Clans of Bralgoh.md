Clans of Bralgogh are informally organized clans and tribes found across the Bralgogh plains. Mostly orc, goliaths, goblin, hobgolin, and bugbear citizens. They have a heavy focus on the worship of ancestors, with most clans having a specific group of ancestors that they worship, intent on bringing them into apotheosis. 

Most clans distrust outsiders, but have certain laws of hospitality and will offer an exchange of gifts, usually requesting small trinkets in return for a place to stay.
