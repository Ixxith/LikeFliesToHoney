This trap is filled with oozing corrosive slime that flow in a constant winding loop in a clockwise direction, the slime passing through a small grate passage on the bottom of the trap, flowing back to the top of part of the trap. Kept active whenever the auction house has valuable goods in storage. 

Trip's Notes: *This is one of my favorite designs. The trap is built in such a way to prevent tampering from intruders. Attack the slimes? The slimes will cluster up and fight back. Try to lead them away? I have built a special pattern in the wall to lure slimes in a circular motion. While the grates could be blocked, finding a material to do so would be quite the feat.*
