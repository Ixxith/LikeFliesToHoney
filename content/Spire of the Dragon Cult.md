The Spire of the Dragon Cult is located in the [[North Iri Desert]] and is a large fortress that protects an oasis and contains the blue dragon [[Laikos]]. Cultists worship Laikos as a god, with the belief that true followers will be reborn as one of his children. Most of the cultists are 3rd generation or later of kidnapped travelers, having spent their whole life growing up in the cult.
