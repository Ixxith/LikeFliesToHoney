The southern portion of the [[North Iri Desert]] is pockmarked with entrances to a deep and extensive network of jungle caverns, filled with exotic animals and monsters, such as Giant Bees, Blemmyes, and Phase Spiders.

Scavenging parties from [[Dolbuz Ghod]] often come here to scavenge valuable resources, with the most valuable being Blackbark, one of the only known sources in the plane for explosives. However retrieving the rare wood is dangerous, as not only does Blackbark only occur in furthest depths of the caverns, but the trees grow on the shores of Death Lake, which is aptly named.
