This admin office is used to handle the local affairs of the Shifting Faces, including information gathering and sorting, finances, sales, and a back office for [[Trick]], the resident expert enchanter.

The office is headed by Master [[Sivis]], who likes thing calm and boring.

The party has recently broken into the office disguised as chimney sweeps with the goal of finding floor plans for the Mighty Boon. They were escorted in by the captain after raising a bit of suspicion. This suspicion was only raised further by a party member claiming to be there on orders of [[Jimmy]]. 

The party then began working on chimney, making as much noise as possible which created an oppertunity as [[Trick]], whose desk holds the plans they need, grew agitated enough to march to [[Sivis]]'s desk and demand a stop be put to the racket.

Just as Master [[Sivis]] entered, so did a giant bee and goose through window opened to let in the air. 

They were able to successfully grab the plans albeit with quite a bit of commotion.
