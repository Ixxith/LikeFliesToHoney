Over a thousand years ago, the Elves ruled most of the plane, taking the lands after the conflicts of the dragons and giants weakened much of the land and the magic in it. They claimed it was in order to restore balance, but eventually most non-Elves found themselves being displaced in the name of "Preseveration of Nature". 

Eventually the elven empire was felled, mostly by a large alliance of the kingdoms of humans.
