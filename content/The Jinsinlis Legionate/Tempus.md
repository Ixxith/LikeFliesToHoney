## God of War

Tempus is a fickle god that changes sides easily, fully embracing his chaotic nature. 

All clergymen of Tempus are known as "Hammers". Each Hammer receives their own ceremonial armor, depending on their rank. Hammers are broken down further into ranks:
- Acolyte: The lowest in the hierarchy, they wear leather jackets and baldrics.
- Stalwart: Priests, who wear chainmail.
- Hardhar: Warrior-priests, who wear breastplates and bracers.
- Arahar: Battle-chaplains, who wear splint mail.
- Rauthat: Swordmasters, who wear plate mail with shoulder spikes.
- Direhar: Guardian priests, who wear full plate.
- Warlyon: High priests, who wear gilded magic plate mail that enable flight.

Worshippers often say "Tempus thanks you" which is used by the deity's faithful in conjunction with the response "and I thank Tempus".

Temples to the Lord of Battle look more like military fortresses than the archetypal temple. They feature barracks, mess halls, armories, and training grounds.

Due to its tendency to have followers and priests on both sides of any engagement, the church of Tempus had no central authority that might support one side or the other exclusively. However within the [[Jinsinlis Legionate]], however, there was a strict hierarchy and chain of command, with followers directed to spend all effort against the demonic invasion.
