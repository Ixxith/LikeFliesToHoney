This Devil trap involves a three headed devil sitting upon a throne. The devil will refuse to let the anyone who enters unless they answer all of his riddles. Should one refuse to answer the riddle, or answer incorrectly, the devil will assign a penalty of yet more riddles. The strength of this devil is unknown but attacking it is unadvised.

Trip's Notes: *Well the expense to summon this thing is a pain, but luckily we only need it around for when the auction house is in use. Luckily it seems to really enjoy asking riddles, and even better, rarely let's anyone through. I consider this a good trap for intruders trying to sneak around the building. It does concern me that someone might be good at riddles enough to beat it, but it is a devil...*
