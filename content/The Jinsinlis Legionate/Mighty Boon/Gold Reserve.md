A small vault used to house excess gold needed to make change for patrons. Many patrons will pay using a valuable item, rather than gold itself, necessitating that the auction house keeps a small reserve of gold to make change, usually holding ten to twenty thousand gold. 

Room in the [[The Mighty Boon]].
