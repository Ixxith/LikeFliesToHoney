As each guest is a valuable customer, the Shifting Faces dare not purge their lost and found lest an angry customer comes looking for a long lost umbrella. Thus the storage has taken over a basement room. Most of the items are ordinary if sometimes expensive objects, but one never knows if some of these items have been *intentionally* left behind.
