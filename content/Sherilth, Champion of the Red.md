A woman with long black hair,  always dressed in heavy armor, has many scars and burns on face, constantly looks stern and disapproving. Has a tendency of scratching her burns when she thinks. Member of the [[Claws of Eryndor]].

Sherilth was met by the party on their way to [[Calcaria]]. She was hunting down a dangerous group from the [[City of Marn]], known as the [[Collectors of Marn]].
